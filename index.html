I’m an ML researcher working on world models and robot learning. I work with Prof. Biwei Huang and Dr. Fan Feng at UC San Diego. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Emory University in May 2026, where I worked with Prof. Liang Zhao on explanation supervision and 3D medical vision–language models.

My research asks how agents can plan and act from learned visual representations with as little supervision as possible:

  • Long-horizon planning in latent world models: label-free subgoal generation on frozen DINOv2 features, executed by inverse-dynamics controllers.
  • Latent action models: learning control from unlabeled video, so a few hundred action labels are enough.
  • Interpretable world models: sparse autoencoders that turn dense visual embeddings into monosemantic features you can inspect and intervene on.
